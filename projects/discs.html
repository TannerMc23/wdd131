Disc Types & Recommendations

Learn about the main types of discs, how they fly, and which ones are best for beginners and experienced players.

Understanding Flight Numbers

Every disc has four flight numbers that describe how it flies:

  • Speed - How fast the disc must be thrown to fly correctly. (1-14)
  • Glide - How well the disc stays in the air. (1-7)
  • Turn - How much the disc curves to the right (for right-handed backhand throws). Higher numbers mean more overstable, lower numbers mean more understable. (1-(-5))
  • Fade - How much the disc fades left at the end of the flight. Higher numbers mean overstable, lower numbers mean more stable. (0-5)

Higher speed does not always mean better - beginners often benefit from slower, more controllable discs. Such as discs with a speed of 1-5.
